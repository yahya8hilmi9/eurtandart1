The Story

Who is Erth?

The brand

ERTH & ART is a Moroccan fashion brand that transforms cultural heritage into contemporary wearable art. Every piece is handcrafted, blending tradition with modern design.

Vision

To represent Morocco on the global fashion scene through authentic craftsmanship, original design, and timeless storytelling.

Why was Erth created?

ERTH was created to preserve Moroccan heritage in a modern way, turning culture, architecture, and craftsmanship into garments that tell meaningful stories.

ERTH was born in the heart of Casablanca's Old Medina, where craftsmanship has been part of everyday life for generations.

Every piece reflects a story.
